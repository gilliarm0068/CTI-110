# How to Make a Sandwich (Robot Instructions)

## Assumptions
- The robot is standing in front of a kitchen counter.
- The robot’s hands are empty.
- All ingredients are on the counter:
  - A loaf of bread inside a sealed plastic bag
  - A jar of peanut butter with the lid on
  - A jar of jelly with the lid on
  - A butter knife
  - A plate
- The robot knows how to grab, twist, pull, and place objects.

## Steps
1. Place the plate on the counter in front of you.
2. Pick up the loaf of bread.
3. Locate the twist tie on the bread bag.
4. Untwist the twist tie until the bag opens.
5. Pull open the plastic bag.
6. Reach inside the bag and grab two slices of bread.
7. Place the two slices of bread on the plate.
8. Put the bread bag down.
9. Pick up the peanut butter jar.
10. Twist the lid counterclockwise until it comes off.
11. Place the lid on the counter.
12. Pick up the knife.
13. Insert the knife into the peanut butter jar.
14. Scoop peanut butter onto the knife.
15. Spread the peanut butter onto one slice of bread.
16. Put the knife down.
17. Pick up the jelly jar.
18. Twist the lid counterclockwise until it comes off.
19. Place the lid on the counter.
20. Pick up the knife again.
21. Insert the knife into the jelly jar.
22. Scoop jelly onto the knife.
23. Spread the jelly onto the other slice of bread.
24. Put the knife down.
25. Pick up the slice with peanut butter.
26. Place it on top of the slice with jelly.
27. Press gently to combine the slices.

## Done When
The robot has created a complete peanut butter and jelly sandwich and placed it on the plate.
